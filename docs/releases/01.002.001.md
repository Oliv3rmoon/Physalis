**Physalis v1.002.001: renamed from Nightshade TV.** Renamed to Physalis; same app, same title ID, your settings stay.

## What changed

- **The name.** The PS5 home screen, the header, the opening, the About page, the update dialog, the notifications and every message say Physalis, in all nine languages, and so does the phone remote's page. In your Plex account's device list the console is Physalis (PS5).
- **Updates.** The app asks [Oliv3rmoon/Physalis](https://github.com/Oliv3rmoon/Physalis/releases) for a newer release. The old address, Oliv3rmoon/Nightshade-TV, forwards there, so Nightshade TV 01.002.000 and older still find this release and **Update now** installs it over themselves.
- **What stays.** The title ID (PPSA99003) and the data folder (`/data/prosperotv`), so it installs over Nightshade TV and keeps your sources, favorites, recent channels, Plex account and settings. The release's files are named as before.

## Install

- `PPSA99003.zip`: choose **Update now** when Nightshade TV or Physalis offers this release, or extract the ZIP and copy its `PPSA99003` folder over your current one (usually `/data/homebrew/PPSA99003`). Your sources, favorites and settings stay.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download. `gh attestation verify PPSA99003.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Physalis` checks that this repository's workflow built it.
