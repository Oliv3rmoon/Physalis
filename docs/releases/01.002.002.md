**Physalis v1.002.002: free public channels with one press.** Channels on a fresh install without typing an address.

## What changed

- **Free public channels.** With no source set up, Live TV's empty screen offers **Square: Free public channels**. It adds [iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/iptv)'s community-maintained public list as an ordinary playlist source and chooses it, so its channels load now and at every start. Physalis still ships no list of its own: the list is added only when you press Square, and it's removed on the Sources tab like any other source. Neither Physalis nor iptv-org hosts the streams, and some may not play.

## Install

- `PPSA99003.zip`: choose **Update now** when Physalis offers this release, or extract the ZIP and copy its `PPSA99003` folder over your current one (usually `/data/homebrew/PPSA99003`). Your sources, favorites and settings stay.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download. `gh attestation verify PPSA99003.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Physalis` checks that this repository's workflow built it.
