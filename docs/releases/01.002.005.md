**Physalis v1.002.005: MPEG-2 channels.** Channels in MPEG-2 play, a channel that can't play says what it is, and a new version is found while Physalis stays on.

## What changed

- **MPEG-2 channels.** Channels and videos in MPEG-2, as many standard-definition broadcast channels are, now play, and so does the older MPEG-1. The PS5's video decoder has no MPEG-2, so Physalis decodes it on the console's processor, up to 1920 × 1080. Interlaced channels are shown field by field to keep their motion, and a 4:3 or 16:9 picture is shown at the shape the channel gives it. In MPEG-TS, HLS, MP4 and Matroska. With **Stats line** on, it says MPEG2.
- **Why a channel didn't play.** A live channel whose video Physalis can't decode said only "The channel uses an unsupported media format." It now says what the channel sent, such as "The channel uses an unsupported media format: the programme has no supported video (stream type 0x10).", and the failure report saved to USB keeps the stream types.
- **New versions while it stays on.** Physalis looked for a new version only when it started. It now looks again every four hours while it stays on, only while you're in the menu, never while a channel or video plays: a check that comes due while you watch waits until you're back. A version you put off with **Later** isn't offered again until Physalis is started again.

## Install

- `PPSA99003.zip`: choose **Update now** when Physalis offers this release, or extract the ZIP and copy its `PPSA99003` folder over your current one (usually `/data/homebrew/PPSA99003`). Your sources, favorites and settings stay.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download. `gh attestation verify PPSA99003.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Physalis` checks that this repository's workflow built it.
