**Physalis v1.002.003: the community in About.** Where to find other Physalis viewers, from the app.

## What changed

- **The community in About.** The About tab now names the community's Discord server, **nightly**, with its address (`discord.gg/pSqgRU3YP9`) and a QR code your phone opens it with. The first time Physalis opens with this version, a card shows the same once, after the opening; any button closes it, and it doesn't come back. In all nine languages.
- **QR codes right to left.** In Arabic and Hebrew the QR codes (pairing a phone, signing in to Plex and now the community) were mirrored with the rest of the screen. They keep their place but are drawn as made, the way a phone expects to read them.

## Install

- `PPSA99003.zip`: choose **Update now** when Physalis offers this release, or extract the ZIP and copy its `PPSA99003` folder over your current one (usually `/data/homebrew/PPSA99003`). Your sources, favorites and settings stay.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download. `gh attestation verify PPSA99003.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Physalis` checks that this repository's workflow built it.
