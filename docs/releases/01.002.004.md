**Physalis v1.002.004: long lists, faster.** Scroll faster, jump to a letter in On demand, and why a channel didn't open in plain words.

## What changed

- **Faster scrolling.** Hold Up or Down and the focus goes faster the longer you hold it: two rows a step after about a second, then four, then eight. In Live TV, Favorites, On demand, Sources and the provider's categories. L2 and R2 still turn a page; held for 3, 5 and 7 seconds they turn two, four and eight pages at a time, and they now turn pages of the provider's categories too (Touchpad on Live TV).
- **Letters in On demand.** In Movies, TV shows and their categories, press Right to reach the letters beside the list, # and A to Z as on Live TV. Up and Down jump to the next letter that has titles, and Left, Cross or Circle go back to the list. Going onto the letters sorts the list Title A–Z. Ported from ProsperoTV, by JMU Technologies.
- **Network errors in plain words.** A channel that doesn't open says why in your language, such as "the channel's server could not be reached in time; it may be offline or blocked on this network (curl 28)", instead of "connection failed (native error 0xFFFFD8D4)". When a channel's server can't be reached, it is no longer asked three times over (about thirty seconds for some channels); Physalis says so after the first try. Some of the public list's channels are on servers that no longer take connections. A channel list that can't be downloaded says why the same way.

## Install

- `PPSA99003.zip`: choose **Update now** when Physalis offers this release, or extract the ZIP and copy its `PPSA99003` folder over your current one (usually `/data/homebrew/PPSA99003`). Your sources, favorites and settings stay.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download. `gh attestation verify PPSA99003.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Physalis` checks that this repository's workflow built it.
