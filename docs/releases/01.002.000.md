**Nightshade TV v1.002.000: Plex.** Your Plex libraries beside your playlists and accounts: sign in on the TV with a code at plex.tv/link, and your Plex servers' movies and TV shows are in On demand.

## What changed

- **Plex as a source.** In **Sources**, **Add Plex** shows a four-character code and a QR code that opens [plex.tv/link](https://plex.tv/link). Enter the code on a phone or computer signed in to Plex, and the account's servers, its own and those shared with it, become sources. Sources then shows the account's name, **Find servers** and **Sign out**.
- **Movies and TV shows from Plex** in **On demand**, each library a category, with seasons, episodes, posters and details, search, Home and Continue.
- **Playing as it is, or through the transcoder.** Files the PS5 decodes play as they are; anything else plays through the server's transcoder over HLS. Playback starts where you left the title on any device, keeps the server told where you are, and marks the title watched at its end.
- **Connections** are tried as Plex's own clients rank them, all at once, and a server gets nothing, not even a poster, until it has answered as itself; plain http only on your own network. A server that does not answer says **Can't be reached** in Sources.
- **Your tokens** are kept as Xtream passwords are and travel only in headers to their own server; the diagnostic log never contains them, settings backups leave Plex servers out, and signing out overwrites them.
- **Every new text** is in all nine languages.

The Plex API is used as [PlxNative](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native) by GLinnik21 (GPL-3.0) documents and uses it. Limits are in the README's [Plex](https://github.com/Oliv3rmoon/Nightshade-TV#plex) section.

Built and checked by the host tests; not yet run on a console with a real Plex server.

## Install

- `PPSA99003.zip`: choose **Update now** when Nightshade TV offers this release, or extract the ZIP and copy its `PPSA99003` folder over your current one (usually `/data/homebrew/PPSA99003`). Your sources, favorites and settings stay.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksum of the download. `gh attestation verify PPSA99003.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Nightshade-TV` checks that this repository's workflow built it.
